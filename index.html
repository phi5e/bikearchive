    Astalla Bici is an on-again, off-again relationship. We know we should stop, but there is always another frame around the corner: rare, beautiful, impossible to pass up. So we bring it home and build it up from the parts we have, or dream about the parts we don’t. Cold steel, grubby broken fingernails, wonderful WD-40, the everlasting search for the right hex key.

    We are a DIY bike garage in Munich, Germany. We build what we like, never for money or for clients, and show the bikes here for posterity.

    But a garage is just a garage. Parts pile up, frames hang from the ceiling, and there’s barely room left to work. Nobody can keep fifteen bikes, and bikes need to be free anyway, like wild mustangs, or some shit. So some are somberly sold, a few keenly kept. We cut our unkempt hair, air out our sweaty, oily shirts and swear that’s the end of it. Then the next one calls, and we come back together in an endless maelstrom of clanking steel, salty sweat and moldy rubber. It’s a cycle, after all.
